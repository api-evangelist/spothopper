# SpotHopper

> SpotHopper is a Milwaukee, Wisconsin restaurant marketing and web technology company, founded in 2015, selling an all-in-one AI-assisted platform to independent restaurants and multi-location groups: website design and hosting, SEO and Google Business Profile syncing, menu/specials/event publishing, automated social posting, email and SMS campaigns, review generation, and link-outs to reservation and ordering partners. SpotHopper publishes NO public API, NO developer portal and NO machine-readable contract; there is nothing here for an agent to call.

Generated 2026-08-29 by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from apis.yml and this repo's probe artifacts. SpotHopper does not publish its own /llms.txt (https://www.spothopperapp.com/llms.txt returns the single-page-application catch-all homepage, HTTP 200, 171,924 bytes — a soft-404, not a document).

## APIs

- No public API. Contract discovery on 2026-08-29 probed www.spothopperapp.com and api.spotapps.co for /openapi.json, /openapi.yaml, /swagger.json, /swagger/v1/swagger.json, /v1/openapi.json, /api-docs, /docs, /redoc, /apidocs and /graphql. Every path returned the marketing homepage; /api and /api/v1 returned a hard HTTP 404. No GraphQL, MCP, A2A agent card, gRPC or WSDL surface was found.
- Despite its name, https://api.spotapps.co is not an API host — it resolves to the same address as the spothopperapp.com apex (34.194.50.185) and serves the same marketing application. spotapps.co is the domain SpotHopper serves customer restaurant websites from.
- Integration posture, in SpotHopper's own words on its FAQ: the platform "automatically syncs your website and menu with Google" and links out "to platforms like OpenTable, Yelp, and UberEats, so guests can make reservations or place orders directly — no integrations needed."
